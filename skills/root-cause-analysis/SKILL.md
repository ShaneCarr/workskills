# Analyze root cause

Build a timeline from source, telemetry, logs, deployment records, and reproducible behavior.
Distinguish correlation, trigger, contributing conditions, detection gaps, and root cause.

Explain the causal chain: what changed or failed, why safeguards did not prevent it, how the impact
propagated, and what evidence supports each link. Identify uncertainty and the measurement needed
to resolve it.

Recommend corrective actions across prevention, detection, mitigation, and recovery. Assign actions
to the failed mechanism, not to vague goals such as "be more careful." Do not blame individuals or
rewrite history to fit the first hypothesis.
