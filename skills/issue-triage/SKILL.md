# Triage an issue

Capture observed behavior, expected behavior, environment, revision, inputs, frequency, impact, and
the smallest known reproduction. Separate reporter statements, verified facts, and hypotheses.

Check for duplicates by behavior and scope, not title alone. Identify likely ownership from the
failing boundary while preserving uncertainty. Classify the next action as close, clarify,
reproduce, investigate, plan, or escalate.

Produce a concise issue update with impact, evidence, missing information, proposed owner, and next
step. Do not assign severity from emotion or claim a root cause from logs that only show timing.
