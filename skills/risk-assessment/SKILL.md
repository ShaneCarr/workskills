# Assess engineering risk

Identify affected users and systems, data and security boundaries, compatibility, operational load,
blast radius, migration, and rollback. For each material risk state the failure mechanism,
triggering conditions, impact, likelihood evidence, detection, and mitigation.

Prioritize irreversible, hard-to-detect, security-sensitive, and wide-blast-radius failures. Do not
inflate routine uncertainty into high risk. Recommend focused validation, staged rollout, flags, or
rollback only when they address a named mechanism.
